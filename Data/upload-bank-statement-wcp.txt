1. Download bank statement in xls format
2. Copy required row-column data in new sheet.
3. Select all row-columns & unmerge cells.
4. Remove empty columns
5. Date & Remark are splited in 2 rows; so extract remark & place it as a next column after Date.
6. Copy-paste Remark as a  value.
7. Apply filter to show blank Amount & delete all those columns
8. In Date column replace month part "/dd/" with "-MMM-" to upload correct date. 30/08/2025: Replace "/08/" with "-Aug-".
9. In Amount column replace "Dr " with "-" & "Cr " with "".
10. In Balance column replace "," with "".
11. Add DR column with formula: =IF(C2<0,C2,0)
12. Add CR column with formula: =IF(C2>0,C2,0)
